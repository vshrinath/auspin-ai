The transition from a successful AI pilot to a production-scale system is where most enterprise initiatives die. It's rarely a failure of the model itself; rather, it's a failure of the surrounding infrastructure—the data plumbing, the governance guardrails, and the human workflows.

In this essay, we explore why this "Integration Gap" exists and how to design for production from Day 1.
