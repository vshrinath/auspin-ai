## The Situation
After a year of hype and a few expensive, underperforming pilots, the organization is retreating into skepticism. "AI doesn't work for us" has become the default stance among key stakeholders.

## What’s Actually Breaking
The organization attempted "General AI" for a "Specific Problem." By trying to solve everything with a chatbot, they solved nothing of value. The disillusionment is a result of mismatched expectations and poor component selection.

## What Changes When This Is Handled Well
- Trust is rebuilt through small, high-certainty wins.
- The organization learns to distinguish between "LLM magic" and "deterministic execution."
- A realistic, disciplined roadmap replaces the former speculative frenzy.

## How Work Typically Begins
A "Reality Check" audit to diagnose why previous pilots failed and where the high-probability value actually resides.
