## The Situation
You have shipped AI features, but they feel like table stakes. Every competitor has the same "Summarize" button. Your product isn't actually becoming more defensible or valuable as more people use it.

## What’s Actually Breaking
The "data flywheels" described in Silicon Valley decks are missing in reality. Data is being consumed to generate outputs, but the *feedback loops*—where usage improves the underlying model or organizational context—are not being captured.

## What Changes When This Is Handled Well
- Every user interaction strengthens your proprietary data moat.
- The product becomes smarter and more personalized with every session.
- You move from providing a generic LLM wrapper to providing a context-rich platform.

## How Work Typically Begins
A data strategy sprint to identify and build the compounding loops missing from your roadmap.
