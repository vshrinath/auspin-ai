## The Situation
Leadership is under intense pressure to "do AI," but the board is asking questions that generic dashboards can’t answer. They want to know the true path to ROI, the specific risk profile of deep integration, and how governance will keep pace with rapid deployment.

## What’s Actually Breaking
The failure isn’t technical; it’s a translation error. Technical teams are reporting on latency and token counts, while the board expects reports on capital efficiency and competitive defensibility. Without a shared framework, trust evaporates and pilots get shelved.

## What Changes When This Is Handled Well
- The board has a clear ROI roadmap that justifies continued investment.
- Governance is baked into the execution, not added as a subsequent hurdle.
- Leadership can communicate a coherent AI strategy that aligns with long-term business goals.
