## The Situation
The "Innovation Lab" has produced several impressive demos, but the business units won’t fund them at scale. The pilots feel like toys rather than tools that can move the needle on the core P&L.

## What’s Actually Breaking
Most innovation initiatives fail to account for the "total cost of production." They solve a narrow technical problem but ignore the massive integration, data-cleaning, and change-management costs required to make the solution profitable in the real world.

## What Changes When This Is Handled Well
- Projects are assessed on "Economic Feasibility" from Day 1.
- Technical success is tightly coupled with unit economic improvement.
- The path from lab to ledger is clearly defined and commercially viable.

## How Work Typically Begins
We run a Feasibility Sprint to pressure-test the unit economics of a specific pilot.
