## The Situation
Service-heavy business models are seeing their margins compressed as clients expect AI-driven efficiency, but the internal "cost-to-serve" remains high because human workflows haven't actually been transformed.

## What’s Actually Breaking
Organizations are layering AI on top of legacy processes rather than redesigning those processes for an AI-native environment. This creates a "shadow cost" where you pay for both the legacy humans and the new (expensive) compute.

## What Changes When This Is Handled Well
- Workflows are redesigned to leverage agents as primary actors, not just assistants.
- Cost-to-serve drops significantly while throughput increases.
- The business model shifts from selling hours to selling outcomes.

## How Work Typically Begins
A rigorous workflow audit to identify where AI is adding cost versus creating value.
