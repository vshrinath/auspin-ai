## The Situation
The technology works in the dev environment, but the employees aren't using it. The "last mile" of adoption has become an impassable wall of friction, confusion, or quiet resistance.

## What’s Actually Breaking
The interface between human and AI is designed as a "chat box" when the work requires a "structured agent." People don't want to prompt; they want to complete tasks. When AI is force-fitted into old UI patterns, productivity actually drops.

## What Changes When This Is Handled Well
- AI is invisible, embedded directly into the "flow of work."
- Training requirements drop because the system anticipates intent.
- Adoption metrics shift from "active users" to "tasks completed autonomously."

Human-in-the-loop workflow mapping to identify the precise friction points killing adoption.
