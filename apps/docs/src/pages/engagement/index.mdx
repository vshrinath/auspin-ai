## How Serious Work Typically Begins

We don't believe in lengthy discovery phases that lead to thick decks. We believe in rapid, technical validation.

### Alignment
We start by aligning your leadership team on technical reality versus business hype. This involves a 2-day workshop to map high-value business objectives to viable AI components.

### Feasibility
Next, we run a 2-week technical sprint to validate the data, the architecture, and the unit economics of a specific initiative. We build just enough to prove it can work in your environment.

### Commitment
Once feasibility is proven, we define the clear roadmap for production, governance, and scale. We don't ask for long-term commitment until the technical and economic logic is undeniable.

We prioritize mutual fit over transaction volume. Our work is practitioner-led and requires active engagement from your executive team.
