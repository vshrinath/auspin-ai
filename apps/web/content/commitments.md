## Business objective driven

Link AI directly to business goals, ensuring initiatives advance growth, efficiency, or market advantage

## ROI focus

Prioritize financial viability, with ROI and cost-to-serve as non-negotiable design criteria

## Strategy aligned

Anchor AI in boardroom priorities and competitive positioning, avoiding tech bets that drain resources

## Phased adoption

Drive enterprise-wide adoption through phased rollouts that deliver wins at every stage

## Managed risks

Establish governance guardrails that protect reputation, compliance, and operational continuity
